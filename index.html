🥗 Diet for Weight Loss (वेज)
  • Sprouts (अंकुरित मूंग) — high fiber, low calorie
  • Chickpeas (चना/छोले) — protein-rich
  • Tofu (टोफू) and paneer (low-fat) (पनीर)
  • Greens: spinach (पालक), broccoli (ब्रोकली)
  • Cucumber (खीरा) and tomato (टमाटर) salad
  • Avoid: fried food, sugary drinks, refined flour (मैदा)
💪 Exercises to Burn Fat
  • Cardio (30–45 min): running, cycling, jump rope
  • Bodyweight circuits: squats, lunges, burpees
  • Core: planks, Russian twists
  • Walk 10,000 steps daily
🥗 Diet for Weight Loss (नॉन-वेज)
  • Chicken breast (चिकन ब्रेस्ट) — grilled, not fried
  • Eggs (अंडे) — boiled, limit yolks if needed
  • Mushrooms (मशरूम) and broccoli (ब्रोकली)
  • Fish (मछली) — grilled or steamed
  • Drink 3–4L water daily
  • Avoid: fried chicken, processed meats, mayo-based sauces
💪 Exercises to Burn Fat
  • HIIT (20–30 min): sprints, kettlebell swings
  • Strength training: squats, deadlifts, bench press
  • Compound moves: mountain climbers
  • NEAT: walk 10,000 steps daily
🍎 Foods for Weight Gain (वेज)
  • Peanuts / peanut butter (मूंगफली) — calorie-dense
  • Full-fat paneer (पनीर) and dairy (दूध)
  • Bananas (केला) and dry fruits (मेवे)
  • Rice (चावल), potatoes (आलू)
  • Sprouts + chickpea salad (अंकुरित मूंग और छोले)
  • Eat 5–6 meals daily with a calorie surplus
🏋️ Exercises for Muscle Gain
  • Compound lifts (4–5x/week): squats, deadlifts, push-ups
  • Progressive overload weekly
  • Light cardio (1–2 sessions/week)
  • 7–9 hours of sleep for recovery
🍗 Foods for Weight Gain (नॉन-वेज)
  • Eggs (अंडे) — whole eggs for calories
  • Chicken thigh (चिकन) — slightly fattier cuts okay
  • Bacon/red meat in moderation (मांस)
  • Whey protein shake + banana + milk
  • Full-fat dairy (दूध, पनीर)
  • Eat 5–6 meals daily (500+ calorie surplus)
🏋️ Exercises for Muscle Gain
  • Compound lifts (4–5x/week): bench press, deadlifts, pull-ups
  • Progressive overload weekly
  • Moderate cardio: 20 min, 1–2x/week
  • Prioritize 7–9 hours sleep
⚖️ Maintain Your Balance (वेज)
  • Keep a balanced plate: protein, fiber, healthy fats
  • Stay hydrated — 3L water daily (पानी)
  • Limit processed/sugary foods
💪 Stay Active
  • Mix of strength training and cardio, 4–5x/week
  • Stretch/mobility work daily
